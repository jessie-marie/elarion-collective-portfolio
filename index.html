About the Collective

The Elarion Collective is my independent practice — the banner I do client work, public-good builds, games, and volunteer sprints under. One person, full stack, from database schema to launch-day event.

I care about software that actually gets used: responsive on real phones, honest about offline limits, and maintainable by whoever inherits it.

What I do best

  • 📊 Dashboards that answer questions, not just display charts
  • 🏛️ Mobile experiences for people standing in a real place
  • 🗄️ Databases, CRMs & automations for small teams
  • 🔮 Creative coding with genuine craft behind it
